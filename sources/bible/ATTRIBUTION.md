# Attribution

The files in this directory are generated by
[`scripts/build-bible.py`](../../scripts/build-bible.py) from three
**public-domain** texts, as exported by [eBible.org](https://ebible.org) in
USFM:

| Field | Text | eBible.org ID |
| --- | --- | --- |
| `ar` | Smith & Van Dyck Arabic Bible (1865), Syrian Mission / American Bible Society | `arb-vd` |
| `kjv` | King James Version, 1769 standardized text | `eng-kjv2006` |
| `web` | World English Bible | `eng-web` |

QQL returns them as `ar`, `en` and `en_web`.

The King James Version is in the public domain everywhere except the United
Kingdom, where letters patent restrict *printing* it, or importing printed
copies, to the Crown's licensed printers. "World English Bible" is a trademark
of eBible.org, which asks that a changed text not carry the name.

## What is carried

The 66 books of the Protestant canon — the canon of the KJV and the Van Dyck —
in English verse numbering, 31,104 verses. The WEB's deuterocanonical books
are not carried. A psalm's title opens its verse 1, as English Bibles number
it. The Jewish Torah and Psalms, in Hebrew numbering, are separate sources:
`sources/torah/` and `sources/psalms/`.

`books.json` lists each book's code, testament, its Arabic name as the Van
Dyck gives it, and the verse count of every chapter.

## Where the texts differ

The build refuses to write unless the texts differ in exactly these places:

| Where | KJV | Van Dyck | WEB | Carried as |
| --- | --- | --- | --- | --- |
| 1 Timothy 6:22, 3 John 1:15 | inside the verse before | a verse of its own | inside the verse before | the verse, with `ar` only |
| Luke 17:36, Acts 8:37, 15:34, 24:7 | present | present | omitted, following the critical text | `web` empty |
| Romans' closing doxology | 16:25–27 | 16:25–27 | after 14:23 | at 16:25–27 in all three |

An untranslated field is `""`, never a missing key.

## Changes from the exports

The words are as exported. USFM markup is removed: Strong's numbers,
footnotes, cross-references, and section headings and introductions, which
are not verse text. Also removed are the Song of Songs' speaker labels and
the KJV's subscriptions to the epistles ("Written to the Hebrews from
Italy…"). The WEB's Romans doxology is moved, as above.

Words the KJV translators supplied (printed in italics) keep their square
brackets, and its paragraph marks (¶) are kept, as eBible's own plain-text
export prints them. Measured against that export, the converter reproduces
the Van Dyck and the KJV word for word, all 31,104 and 31,102 verses.
