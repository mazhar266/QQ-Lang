# Attribution

The psalm files in this directory are generated by
[`scripts/build-psalms.py`](../../scripts/build-psalms.py) from five
**public-domain** texts, as exported by [eBible.org](https://ebible.org) in
USFM:

| Field | Text | eBible.org ID |
| --- | --- | --- |
| `he` | Hebrew Masoretic text | `hbo` |
| `en` | JPS 1917, the Jewish Publication Society's translation | `engjps` |
| `ar` | Smith & Van Dyck Arabic Bible (1865), Syrian Mission / American Bible Society | `arb-vd` |
| `kjv` | King James Version, 1769 standardized text | `eng-kjv2006` |
| `web` | World English Bible | `eng-web` |

QQL returns them as `he`, `en`, `ar`, `en_kjv` and `en_web`.

The King James Version is in the public domain everywhere except the United
Kingdom, where letters patent restrict *printing* it, or importing printed
copies, to the Crown's licensed printers. "World English Bible" is a trademark
of eBible.org, which asks that a changed text not carry the name.

## Numbering

The verses are the **Hebrew** text's, in which a psalm's title is a verse of
its own: 2527 verses, where English Bibles have 2461. Only the Book of Psalms
is carried. `english` lists the verse number(s) English Bibles give each
verse.

## Changes from the exports

The words of every verse are as exported. What the build changes is where
they are divided:

- **All four translations are re-cut to the Hebrew verses.** They arrive in
  English numbering.
  - JPS 1917 carries the Hebrew verse numbers inline, "(3-1) … (3-2) …", and
    is divided exactly there; the markers are removed.
  - Van Dyck, the KJV and the WEB mark a psalm's title apart from verse 1, and
    are divided at the title.
  - Where a title spans two Hebrew verses (Psalms 51, 52, 54 and 60), those
    three fill the first and leave the second empty.
- **JPS, Psalm 70.** The export has no Hebrew verse markers for this psalm;
  its title is divided after the words "to make memorial.", by an explicit
  rule in the build.
- **JPS, five psalms.** The Psalter's book headings ("BOOK II") sit at the
  start of Psalms 1, 42, 73, 90 and 107 and are removed. They are headings,
  not text.
- **WEB, Psalm 119.** The export marks the 22 acrostic headings (`ALEPH` …
  `TAV`) as psalm titles. They are not carried.
- **USFM markup is removed.** That covers Strong's numbers, footnotes and
  word-level tags. Words the KJV translators supplied (printed in italics)
  keep their square brackets, as eBible's own plain-text export prints them.
  The converter reproduces that export exactly for Van Dyck and the KJV, all
  2461 verses.

The build refuses to write unless every Hebrew verse is filled and every
translation agrees with the Hebrew on where *Selah* and *David* fall.
