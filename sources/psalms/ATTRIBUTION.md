# Attribution

The psalm files in this directory are generated by
[`scripts/build-psalms.py`](../../scripts/build-psalms.py) from three
**public-domain** texts, as exported by [eBible.org](https://ebible.org) in
its verse-per-line (VPL) format:

| Field | Text | eBible.org ID |
| --- | --- | --- |
| `ar` | Smith & Van Dyck Arabic Bible (1865), Syrian Mission / American Bible Society | `arb-vd` |
| `kjv` | King James Version, 1769 standardized text | `eng-kjv2006` |
| `web` | World English Bible, 2020 stable text | `eng-web` |

QQL returns `kjv` as a record's `en` and `web` as its `en_web`.

The King James Version is in the public domain everywhere except the United
Kingdom, where letters patent restrict *printing* it, or importing printed
copies, to the Crown's licensed printers. "World English Bible" is a trademark
of eBible.org, which asks that a changed text not carry the name.

## Changes from the exports

Only the Book of Psalms is carried. The verse text is otherwise as exported,
with one exception:

- **WEB, Psalm 119.** The export keeps the psalm's 22 acrostic headings
  (`ALEPH` … `TAV`), though it strips every other section title, and puts all
  but the first at the *end* of the previous stanza's last verse — 119:8 reads
  "Don't utterly forsake me. BETH". They are headings, not translation text,
  and neither of the other two texts carries them, so the build removes them.
  The words of every verse are unchanged.

The KJV's square brackets, which mark words the translators supplied (printed
in italics), are kept, just as the Quran translation keeps its own.
