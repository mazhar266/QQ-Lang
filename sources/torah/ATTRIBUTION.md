# Attribution

The files in this directory are generated by
[`scripts/build-torah.py`](../../scripts/build-torah.py) from five
**public-domain** texts, as exported by [eBible.org](https://ebible.org) in
USFM:

| Field | Text | eBible.org ID |
| --- | --- | --- |
| `he` | Hebrew Masoretic text | `hbo` |
| `en` | JPS 1917, the Jewish Publication Society's translation | `engjps` |
| `ar` | Smith & Van Dyck Arabic Bible (1865), Syrian Mission / American Bible Society | `arb-vd` |
| `kjv` | King James Version, 1769 standardized text | `eng-kjv2006` |
| `web` | World English Bible | `eng-web` |

QQL returns them as `he`, `en`, `ar`, `en_kjv` and `en_web`.

The King James Version is in the public domain everywhere except the United
Kingdom, where letters patent restrict *printing* it, or importing printed
copies, to the Crown's licensed printers. "World English Bible" is a trademark
of eBible.org, which asks that a changed text not carry the name.

## What is carried

The five books of Moses — Genesis, Exodus, Leviticus, Numbers, Deuteronomy —
in the **Hebrew** text's verse numbering: 5,853 verses. The same books sit in
`sources/bible/` in English numbering, as part of the Christian Bible.

The two numberings disagree in ten places, where a verse or two moves across
a chapter boundary (Hebrew Genesis 32:1 is English 31:55), and once inside a
verse: English Numbers 26:1 is Hebrew 25:19 and 26:1. `english` lists the
`(chapter, verse)` English Bibles give each verse.

## Changes from the exports

The words of every verse are as exported. What the build changes is where
they are divided:

- **All four translations are re-cut to the Hebrew verses.** They arrive in
  English numbering.
  - JPS 1917 carries the Hebrew verse numbers inline, "(32-1) …", and is
    divided exactly there; the markers are removed.
  - Van Dyck, the KJV and the WEB move whole verses.
  - Numbers 26:1 is divided after "And it came to pass after the plague,",
    "After the plague," and `ثم بعد الوبإ`. Each cut is checked against the
    text.
- **The Ten Commandments.** In Exodus 20 and Deuteronomy 5, JPS numbers by the
  "lower" accents, joining the sixth to ninth commandments into one Hebrew
  verse. The Hebrew text here keeps each a verse of its own, as English Bibles
  do. In those two chapters JPS's markers are set aside and its verses keep
  their numbers.
- **USFM markup is removed.** That covers Strong's numbers, footnotes and
  word-level tags. Words the KJV translators supplied keep their square
  brackets, as eBible's own plain-text export prints them. The Hebrew keeps
  the Masoretic paragraph marks (`פ`, `ס`) the export carries.

The build refuses to write unless every Hebrew verse is filled in all five
texts. The Hebrew and English chapter counts must differ in exactly the
expected 19 chapters, and *Moses*, *Aaron*, *Pharaoh* and *Jacob* must fall in
the same Hebrew verses in every text.
